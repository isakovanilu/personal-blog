## Getting Started Safely

### Beginner's Approach
- Start with walking
- Progress to walk-run intervals
- Build up to continuous running
- Listen to your body signals

### Essential Preparation
- Invest in good running shoes
- Warm up properly
- Stay hydrated
- Get adequate rest

## Training Structure

### Weekly Schedule
- Monday: Easy 5K
- Tuesday: Moderate 5K
- Wednesday: Easy 5K
- Thursday: Moderate 5K
- Friday: Easy 5K
- Saturday: Long run (6-8K)
- Sunday: Rest or light walk

### Intensity Guidelines
- Easy days: 60-70% effort
- Moderate days: 70-80% effort
- Long run: 65-75% effort
- Always listen to your body
