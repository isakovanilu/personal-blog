# DEKA Ultra: The Ultimate 50-Zone Endurance Challenge

As an endurance athlete and fitness enthusiast, I'm excited to share details about one of the most challenging and comprehensive fitness events available today - the DEKA Ultra. This extraordinary event combines endurance running with functional fitness in a unique and demanding format.

## Understanding DEKA Ultra

DEKA Ultra combines:
- 25K total running distance
- 50 functional fitness zones
- Multiple competition categories
- Comprehensive test of fitness domains

## Event Breakdown

### Running Components
- 5 circuits of 5K each
- Total distance: 25K (15.5 miles)
- Varied terrain
- Strategic pacing required

### Fitness Zones
- 10 unique zones per circuit
- 50 total zone completions
- Functional movement patterns
- Strength and skill challenges

## Competition Categories

### Elite Division
- Men's time cap: 3:45:00
- Women's time cap: 4:15:00
- Strict movement standards
- World Championship qualifying opportunity

### Age Group Division
- 9-hour time cap
- Multiple age categories
- Modified movement options
- Team competition available

### Ruck Division
- Additional weight carried
- Special category rules
- Modified time requirements
- Unique challenge element
